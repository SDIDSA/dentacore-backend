Frequently asked

04
Where does my data live?

On Sera's secured servers, isolated per clinic and backed up automatically every night. Your clinic's data is never shared with other clinics, and you can export it at any time.

What happens if the internet goes out?

Nothing dramatic. The app keeps working fully offline; changes made while disconnected are queued safely and replayed automatically in order when the connection returns.

Which platforms are supported?

Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). The backend runs on Sera's hosted servers — there is no server component to install.

How does the trial work?

Download the installer, install, sign in. You get 30 days with every feature unlocked — no credit card. Keep using the Starter or Clinic plan afterwards.

Can my team use it at the same time?

Yes. Role-based accounts (admin, dentist, receptionist) work concurrently, and every sensitive action is recorded in a tamper-evident audit log.
